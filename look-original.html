Getting started

What should I include when I get in touch?

A little detail up front makes the first conversation about your project much more useful.

When you email Craig, it helps to include:

  1. Where the work is. The property address or the suburb in Christchurch.
  2. What you'd like done. A few sentences describing the building or carpentry work you have in mind.
  3. Anything you already have. Plans, drawings, measurements or photos of the space.
  4. Your timing. When you'd ideally like the work to start, and any dates that matter to you.
  5. How to reach you. Your phone number and the best time to call.
